# X Coder 4.7

- AI now handles greetings, general questions, casual/off-topic conversation, and self-description naturally.
- Coding/project work remains the primary specialization.
- AI status UI now shows concise activity summaries such as understanding the request, checking relevant context, preparing edits, and validating changes. These are progress summaries, not hidden chain-of-thought.
- Composer placeholder shortened to `Message X Coder`.
- Thinking indicator now includes a compact title + detail line.
- Mobile AI messages, proposal headers, diffs, and action buttons were tightened to avoid clipping/overflow.
- AI completion messages now summarize the files that were actually changed.
- Undo and Redo copy/status behavior is clearer.
- PWA/service worker version bumped to 4.8.0.
